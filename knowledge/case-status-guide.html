What each case status means

Every application gets an 8 digit case number. It's printed at the top of every letter from the Office of Benefit Services. To check a status by phone, callers need the case number and the ZIP code on the application.

Received

The application arrived and is waiting to be assigned. Nothing is needed from the applicant.

In review

A reviewer is working on the application. For disability claims this includes the medical review. Nothing is needed unless a letter asks for more information.

Additional information needed

The review is paused until the applicant sends something, such as a missing document. The request letter lists what's needed and the due date. If the due date passes, the application may be denied, so callers should send the document or ask a specialist for more time.

Decision issued

A decision was made and a letter was mailed. The letter explains the decision, the monthly amount if approved, and how to appeal.

Payment scheduled

The claim was approved and the first payment has a date. Payments go to the bank account on file.

Appeals

Applicants who disagree with a decision have 60 days from the date on the decision letter to ask for an appeal. Appeals can be filed at benefits.example or with a specialist.
