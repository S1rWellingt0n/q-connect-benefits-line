Contact options and hours

Automated phone help

The benefits help line answers program questions and checks case status at any time, every day.

Benefits specialists

Specialists take calls Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM Central time, except federal holidays. Callers can ask for a specialist at any point in the call. Outside those hours, callers should call back during business hours.

Specialists can change a mailing address or bank account, extend a due date for documents, start an appeal, and explain a decision letter.

Online

At benefits.example, applicants can apply, upload documents, check status and update contact details.

Privacy

The help line never asks for a full Social Security number, a bank account number or a password. Callers verify a case with the case number and ZIP code only.
