Benefit programs overview

The Office of Benefit Services is a fictional agency used for this demo. It runs three programs: retirement benefits, disability benefits and survivor benefits.

Retirement benefits

Workers can apply for retirement benefits starting at age 62. Applying before full retirement age lowers the monthly amount for life. Full retirement age is 67 for anyone born in 1960 or later.

Apply online at benefits.example, or by mail. Most retirement applications are decided within 30 days of receiving all documents.

Disability benefits

Disability benefits are for workers who can't work for at least 12 months because of a medical condition. Every disability application gets a medical review, which looks at the records from the applicant's doctors.

A disability decision usually takes 3 to 5 months. The medical review is the longest step.

Survivor benefits

When a worker dies, a surviving spouse, a child under 18, or a dependent parent may get survivor benefits. Survivors should apply as soon as possible, because benefits can only be paid back up to 6 months before the application date.

Survivor applications need a death certificate and proof of the relationship, such as a marriage or birth certificate.

Documents

Applicants can upload documents at benefits.example or mail copies to Office of Benefit Services, PO Box 1000, Example City. Don't mail original documents unless a letter asks for them.
